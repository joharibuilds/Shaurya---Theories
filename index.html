The sky is not a live video. The sky is a very old photo album. We are looking at the past, not the present. If a star 10,000 light years away died yesterday, we will still see it for the next 10,000 years. So what is the point of looking at the sky? We are seeing a history that no longer exists.

Chapter 2 - Why Do Stars Twinkle? Science says stars twinkle because of our atmosphere. If that was true, planets would also twinkle. But planets do not twinkle, only stars do. Why? Because planets are close to us, their light is fresh and strong. Stars are far away, their light is very old and tired. After traveling for thousands of years, the light becomes weak and starts shaking. That shaking is what we call twinkling. Twinkling is not wind. Twinkling is tired light. It is like an old man who has walked for 1000 kilometers, his legs will shake. Light also shakes after a long journey.

The night sky is full of dead stars whose light has not yet finished its journey. We are living under a sky of ghosts. Every night we sleep under dead lights.

Darkness is not empty, darkness is the proof of death.

Chapter 5 - Why Do Stars Make You Feel Something Deep? When you look at stars, you feel something strange and peaceful. Why? Because you are made of stars. We are born from dead stars, and we will become dead stars for someone else. We are not under the stars, we are becoming stars.
