THEORY 01 / PHYSICS OF EXISTENCE2026

Nothing is Stable.
Everything Vibrates.

My first and foundational theory is that absolute stability is a myth. Human eyes are deceived by macro-level appearances. We look at a mountain and call it stable, we look at a building and call it permanent, but this is a failure of our perception, not a fact of reality.

At the quantum level, as proven by modern physics, no particle is ever at rest. Electrons are in constant orbit, atoms vibrate with thermal energy even at zero degrees. This is called Zero-Point Energy. Even what appears to be solid matter is actually 99.999% empty space filled with vibrating energy fields.

The Earth itself is a perfect example. We feel we are standing still, but we are on a sphere rotating at 1670 km per hour at the equator, revolving around the Sun at 107,000 km per hour, while the entire solar system moves around the Milky Way at 828,000 km per hour. There is no moment of rest in the universe.

Human life follows the same law. Our heart beats 100,000 times a day, our lungs expand, our cells divide and die every second. If vibration stops, we call it death. Therefore, I conclude that life IS vibration. Our search for a stable job, stable life, stable relationship is against the law of nature. The most successful people are not those who find stability, but those who learn to dance with instability and use the vibration as momentum.

Chapter 1 - The Illusion: Our brain creates the illusion of stability to make life simple to process. But science proves it false.

Chapter 2 - The Proof: From Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle to Brownian Motion, physics has proven that everything jiggles. Nothing can be measured to be perfectly still.

Chapter 3 - The Philosophy: If everything vibrates, then change is not an enemy. Change is the default state. Anxiety comes when we try to stop the vibration.

Final Conclusion: Stop chasing stability. Chase adaptability. The universe rewards those who vibrate at a higher frequency - more energy, more learning, more movement. To be alive is to vibrate. To be stable is to be dead.

THEORY 02 / HUMAN BIOLOGY

The Body is Full of Micro-Holes.

My second theory challenges the idea of the human body as a closed solid object. We are taught in school that skin is a protective barrier. My observation is the opposite - skin is not a barrier, it is a filter full of millions of holes.

The average human body has approximately 5 million hair follicles and over 20,000 sweat pores per square inch on our palms. If you look at human skin under a 100x microscope, it does not look smooth. It looks like the surface of the moon - full of craters, pits, valleys, and openings. Each of these holes has a purpose.

First, they are breathing holes. We don't just breathe from our nose. Our skin respires. Second, they are sensory antennas. Each hair follicle is connected to a nerve ending. The small hairs on our hand stand up when we sense danger - they are like ancient sensors. Third, they are communication portals. We release pheromones, sweat, oils, and heat through these holes to communicate with other humans subconsciously.

This means our body is not separate from the environment. We are in constant exchange. Dust, air, light, bacteria enter us through these holes, and we release ourselves into the world through them. We are not individuals in a box, we are like a sponge constantly exchanging with the universe. This is why location affects mood, why clean air makes us happy - because we are porous beings.

My theory is that diseases also enter through the malfunctioning of these holes, and healing should focus on cleaning these micro-holes, not just taking medicine inside.

THEORY 03 / NEURO-GUT THEORY

Our Stomach is Our Real Brain.

This is my most revolutionary theory. The world says we think from our head. I say we think from our gut. And modern neuroscience is slowly proving me right.

In medical science, there is a term called the Enteric Nervous System (ENS). It is a massive network of 500 million neurons lining our stomach and intestines. That is more neurons than in the spinal cord of many animals. It is so complex that scientists call it the "Second Brain". I call it the FIRST brain.

Why do I call it the first brain? Three reasons. One, evolutionarily it is older. Primitive organisms had a gut before they had a head-brain. Two, chemically it is more powerful. It manufactures 95% of the body's serotonin, which controls happiness, and 50% of dopamine. Your mood is literally made in your stomach, not in your head. Three, intuitively it is faster.

Have you ever noticed? When you are nervous for an exam, you don't get a headache, you get a stomach ache. When you fall in love, you don't feel it in your head, you feel butterflies in your stomach. When you sense danger, your gut tightens. This is not poetry, this is biology. Your gut brain sends signals to your head brain via the Vagus Nerve, which is the longest nerve in the body, and it takes only 100 milliseconds.

The head brain is logical, slow, and full of doubt. It calculates. The stomach brain is intuitive, fast, and always true. It knows. In business, in relationships, in life-threatening situations, people who follow their gut feeling survive and win. My theory for future success is simple: Feed your stomach brain with good food, clean water, and good thoughts, and it will make your head brain a millionaire. The stomach is not for digestion only, it is for decision.
