THEORY NO. 1 - INDEPENDENT ESSAY

The Cosmic Hypothesis Theory

Introduction: We have been taught that the universe is everything that exists. However, modern astrophysics reveals that we can observe only about five percent of the universe. The remaining ninety-five percent is composed of dark matter and dark energy, which we cannot see or touch. This raises a fundamental question: what if the universe we know is not the whole of existence, but merely a small component of a much larger cosmic structure?

1. The Core Hypothesis

The Cosmic Hypothesis Theory proposes that every black hole is a gateway to a new universe. When a massive star dies and collapses under its own gravity, it forms a singularity - a point of infinite density. This description is identical to the way our own universe began during the Big Bang. This similarity is not a coincidence.

The theory states that a black hole and the Big Bang are the same event, viewed from two different perspectives. From the outside, an observer sees a black hole that destroys matter and light. From the inside, an observer experiences a Big Bang that creates space, time, and matter.

2. The Nested Universe Model

If this hypothesis is correct, then our universe, containing two trillion galaxies, is actually existing inside the black hole of a parent universe. That parent universe is itself located inside the black hole of an even larger universe. This creates an infinite chain, similar to a Russian nesting doll, where each universe contains billions of smaller universes and is contained within a larger one.

We are not living in the universe. We are living in a universe, inside another universe, infinitely.

3. Scientific Evidence Supporting This Idea

A) The Information Paradox: In physics, information can never be truly destroyed. If a black hole destroys information, the fundamental laws of physics collapse. But if a black hole transfers that information into a new, baby universe, the law of conservation is perfectly preserved.

B) The Expansion of the Universe: Our universe is continuously expanding. The theory explains why. As the parent black hole in the higher universe consumes more matter and grows larger, the space inside it, which is our universe, is forced to stretch and expand.

C) The Absence of an Edge: We have never been able to find the edge of our universe. According to this theory, there is no edge to find. The edge is the event horizon of the parent black hole, a boundary we can never cross or observe from within.

Final Conclusion: The Cosmic Hypothesis Theory removes human arrogance. We are not the center of existence. We are a small, beautiful, and accidental phenomenon occurring inside a cosmic graveyard that is, in reality, a cosmic nursery continuously giving birth to new universes.

THEORY NO. 2 - INDEPENDENT ESSAY

Ghosts, Paranormal Events and The Reality as a Software Update Theory

Introduction: Every single culture on Earth, from ancient times to the modern era, has reported the existence of ghosts. Science often dismisses this as superstition or imagination. But what if ghosts are not supernatural at all, but purely technological? What if paranormal events are not magic, but simply system errors?

1. The Core Hypothesis

This theory assumes that reality is not physical, but computational. Our entire universe is a highly advanced simulation, like an incredibly realistic video game, running on a system far beyond human understanding. This system is managed by a higher intelligence, which different cultures call God, the Architect, or the System Administrator.

Just like any software, this simulation requires regular updates. New species, new physical laws, and new timelines are patched into the system continuously. What we perceive as evolution and the passage of time is simply the process of the software running and updating itself.

2. What Are Ghosts?

When you update your smartphone, some old cache files are often left behind. These are useless files that were not properly deleted during the update. They occupy memory and sometimes cause the phone to malfunction or lag.

Ghosts are the residual cache files of reality. When a human being dies, their consciousness, which is essentially a complex pattern of data and information, is supposed to be deleted from the main server. However, if this deletion process fails during a major reality update, a fragment of their data remains stuck at a specific geographical location.

This is why ghosts are always seen in the same house, the same fort, or the same stretch of road. They are not wandering souls. They are corrupted data files stuck in a loop, endlessly replaying the final moments of their existence because they were not cleared from the system.

3. Explanation of Other Paranormal Events

A) Deja Vu: Deja vu is a rendering glitch. The system accidentally loads the same moment twice. You feel as if you have already lived this exact moment because, in a literal sense, the system has loaded the same file twice in a row.

B) Why Ghosts Appear at 3 AM: Most paranormal activity is reported at night, particularly around 3 AM. This is because the global system load is at its lowest when the majority of humans are asleep. The system utilizes this low-traffic period to perform background maintenance and error correction, similar to how a computer runs antivirus scans at night. During this maintenance, hidden glitches and leftover files become visible.

C) The Mandela Effect: Thousands of people around the world clearly remember a different version of history. This is not a case of false memory. This is a patch update. The System Administrator edited a historical event file in the main database, but some users still retain the memory of the old version in their local cache.

Final Conclusion: We are not haunted by spirits from another world. We are haunted by outdated code from our own world. The existence of ghosts is proof that reality is not perfect. It is programmed, and every program, no matter how advanced, contains bugs.
